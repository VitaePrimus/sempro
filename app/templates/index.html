What M&S CORNERSTONE does

One contractor. More of the project covered.

From a focused repair to a room-wide renovation, every job starts with a clear look at what the home actually needs.

01

Interior & exterior painting

Preparation, repairs, crisp edges, and durable finishes for rooms, trim, siding, and exterior details.

02

Bathrooms & kitchens

Cosmetic updates and larger remodels, including tile, finish work, fixture coordination, and wall repair.

03

Flooring & tile

Careful layout and installation for practical, polished floors and tiled surfaces built for daily use.

04

Drywall & repairs

Meet the owner
